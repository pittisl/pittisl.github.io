        # Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)
        HRI is defined as the study of how humans and robots communicate and collaborate to perform tasks safely and effectively. Our research leverages AI to make assistive robots usable in everyday tasks by people with profound motor impairments, such as ALS, spinal cord injuries or Parkinson's disease, while keeping the physical and cognitive burden on the user low. We personalize robot behaviors from users' natural language feedback, and let users direct robots with their eye gaze, predicting the intended task from egocentric gaze in a way that tolerates the abnormal gaze patterns that come with motor degeneration.
