Collaboration

The hospitals, schools, universities and companies the Center works with.

The Center’s model only works if it reaches beyond itself. Inside Pitt and UPMC it draws on hospitals and schools whose strengths it needs and does not duplicate; outside, it works with universities, medical schools and companies whose expertise carries a particular project further than the Center could alone.
