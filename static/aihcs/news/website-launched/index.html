Center website launched

The Center for Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Computing and Systems now has a website. It introduces the Center’s background, vision and mission, its members and leadership, the six research areas that span the computing system stack, the clinical domains the work is grounded in, the Center’s collaboration partners across the University of Pittsburgh, UPMC and beyond, and its education program for undergraduate and graduate students.

News about the Center’s activities, seminars and opportunities will be posted here as they are announced.
